CopilotKit is an open-source framework that connects your app to AI agents. Add chat, interactive UI, and human approvals with your choice of agent backend.

[Get started](/quickstart) or [choose your agent backend](/#explore-by-ai-backend).

## Add Intelligence when your agent meets real users

CopilotKit’s open-source framework connects your agent to the UI. CopilotKit Intelligence adds persistent conversations, inspection, learning, and channels around that interaction. Start with one capability and keep your agent stack.

### Rich Threads: conversations and app state

Keep the supported messages, tool results, interactive UI, and state users need to return to a report, approval, or conversation. Rich Threads complement your agent framework’s persistence; keep your existing backend. [Start with free Threads](/threads). Free plan usage and retention limits apply.

### Channels: your agent where work happens

Connect your agent to Slack through managed Intelligence connections or a supported direct adapter. Teams has separate availability and setup requirements. [Bring your agent to Slack](/slack).

### Self-Improving Agents & Product Learning

Automatic Learning turns supported agent interaction history into insights and proposed Skills. Review the evidence, publish useful Skills, and evaluate the changes in your agent. Broader learning from interactions throughout your product is a separate demo or early-access conversation. [Follow a learning workflow](/solutions/self-improving-agents).

### Product analytics: understand what happened

Inspect captured conversations and available activity, errors, tool usage, token usage, and duration. Available views depend on captured data and your plan. [Explore product analytics](https://www.copilotkit.ai/copilotkit-intelligence#analytics-insights).

### Choose where Intelligence runs

Managed Cloud stores thread data in the managed service. Self-hosted Intelligence runs with your infrastructure and database. This is a separate deployment choice from self-hosting the open-source frontend and runtime. [Compare self-hosted requirements](/intelligence/self-hosting).

Try interactive chat and agent UI patterns in the Dojo. [Explore interactive demos](https://dojo.showcase.copilotkit.ai/?integration=langgraph-python&demo=beautiful-chat).
