You want to see which Intelligence plan you are on, and change it. Open plan management in the [cloud-hosted](/intelligence/managed-intelligence-platform) web app.

![The cloud-hosted Intelligence plan page.](/images/cloud-hosted/cloud-hosted-plans.png)

The plan names are:

- **Developer.** The no-cost plan.
- **Team.** A paid plan for a group on cloud-hosted Intelligence.
- **Team Self-hosted.** The plan for a self-hosted deployment. You choose it on the cloud-hosted account. The deployment uses your identity provider.
- **Enterprise.** The plan for a larger deployment.

A new organization chooses Developer or a paid plan during sign-in. You can change the plan later on this page.

The page shows the limits for your plan, including how long threads are kept and whether self-hosted deployment is included.

Some capabilities appear as early access. Those capabilities follow the early-access program.

When you outgrow cloud-hosted, move to [self-hosted Intelligence](/intelligence/self-hosting) on the Team Self-hosted plan or the Enterprise plan. Compare the two deployments on the [architecture page](/intelligence/intelligence-platform).
