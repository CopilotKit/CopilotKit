## Overview

Every organization starts on **Developer**, which is free and includes Rich Threads, User Memory, Automatic Learning, and Product Analytics. Move up when your team or your traffic needs it.

<Image
  src="/images/cloud-hosted/cloud-hosted-plans.png"
  alt="The CopilotKit pricing page. Developer is free, Pro and Team are paid, and Enterprise is a custom plan for production at scale."
  width={2000}
  height={1246}
  className="my-8 w-full rounded-xl border border-[var(--border)]"
/>

Developer is one seat. Move to **Pro** when you are building it out, **Team** when the whole team is in, and **Enterprise** when your agents run at scale or must run in your own infrastructure.
