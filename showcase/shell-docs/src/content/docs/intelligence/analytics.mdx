You want to see what people do with your agent after the chat ends. Analytics reads the threads Intelligence already stores and shows that usage for your project.

Open the [cloud-hosted project](/intelligence/managed-intelligence-platform) for your app. Analytics for that project is there.

In that project you can:

- Watch usage as it happens.
- Review how conversations change over time.
- Query the stored interaction data.
- Send traces to your own observability tool with OTLP.

Analytics is included when the app is connected to Intelligence. The [quickstart](/intelligence/quickstart) makes that connection with the CLI. It signs you in and selects the project. Your existing app and agent stay in place.
