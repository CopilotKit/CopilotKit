A Trajectory records a session in your app. It can include clicks, form edits, page changes, and network requests.

CopilotKit Intelligence stores these events and links related agent conversations. You can inspect what a person did before, during, and after a conversation.

<Callout type="info" title="Early access with our engineering team">
  Trajectories are in early access for proofs of concept with our engineering team. If you need help with access, [book time with our engineers](https://www.copilotkit.ai/talk-to-an-engineer).
</Callout>

Start with the [quickstart](/intelligence/trajectories-quickstart) to record your first session in self-hosted Intelligence.

## How Trajectories relate to Automatic Learning

Trajectories provide evidence about how people use your app. [Automatic Learning](/learning) analyzes evidence and proposes Skills for you to review.

Capture alone does not start an analysis or change an agent. To use a Trajectory as evidence, add it to a Learning Space. See [Assign browser activity to a Learning Space](/learning#assign-browser-activity-to-a-learning-space).

After you review and publish a Skill, [Skill delivery](/intelligence/learned-skills) makes it available to an agent.

[User Memory](/intelligence/memories) stores facts about a person across conversations. It is a separate feature. You do not need User Memory or Automatic Learning to record and inspect a Trajectory.
