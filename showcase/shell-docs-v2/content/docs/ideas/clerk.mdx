>
  "Select your SDK" in the sidebar, and "Available in other SDKs" beside the
  page.
</IdeaReference>

- The select is labelled, so nobody misses that it is a choice.
- The rail shows every SDK as a logo tile; ones without this page are greyed.
- The quickstart leads with a prompt to hand to a coding agent.

## For CopilotKit

<ClerkMock />

Click a logo in the rail to switch frameworks without opening a menu.

## Why it fits

- Partner logos sit on every page, which is good for co-marketing.
- Readers learn the docs cover other frameworks without hunting for it.

## Watch out

- The rail competes with the table of contents on narrow screens.
- With 22 frameworks, show only the ones that have this page.
