**Before you go:**
- The sun sets around 5 pm now. Start the Zirbenweg by noon so you catch the last lift down at 16:45.
- Above about 1,800 m the trail can be icy in the shade after a cold night. Wear proper shoes and check the lift webcams in the morning.
- Hut opening hours thin out in late autumn. Check before you count on lunch up there.
