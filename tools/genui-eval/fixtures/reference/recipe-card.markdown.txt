### How to make them (thick & fluffy)

1. **Mix the dry ingredients.** Whisk the flour, baking powder, sugar and salt in a large bowl.
2. **Add the wet ingredients.** Whisk the milk, eggs and melted butter together, pour them into the flour and stir only until no dry flour is left. A few lumps are fine. Overmixing makes the pancakes tough.
3. **Let it rest for 10 minutes.** The baking powder starts working and the batter thickens slightly.
4. **Cook.** Heat a lightly buttered pan over medium heat. Pour in about 60 ml of batter per pancake. Flip when bubbles cover the top and the edges look set, after about 2 minutes, then cook 1 to 2 minutes more.

Keep the finished pancakes in the oven at 80 °C, loosely covered, while you cook the rest. Altogether it takes about 35 minutes.

**For thin Palatschinken:** whisk the flour and milk until smooth, beat in the eggs and let the batter rest for 20 minutes. Swirl a thin layer into a hot buttered pan and cook for about a minute per side.

**Toppings:** maple syrup, fresh berries or sliced banana, powdered sugar, Nutella, or apricot jam for Palatschinken.
