### Practical details

- **Where to stay:** Baixa or Chiado. Both days start within a short walk or tram ride from there.
- **Getting to Belém:** take the train from Cais do Sodré (about 10 minutes) or tram 15E from Praça do Comércio (about 25 minutes). Back from LX Factory, a taxi or ride-hail to Bairro Alto takes about 15 minutes.
- **Book ahead:** dinner at Clube de Fado, a Belém Tower slot, and Jerónimos tickets online to skip the queue.
- **Sunday is a public holiday.** 1 November is All Saints' Day in Portugal. The big sights normally stay open, but some shops and restaurants close or keep shorter hours.
- **Shoes:** Lisbon is steep and its stone pavements get slippery, so wear comfortable shoes with grip.
