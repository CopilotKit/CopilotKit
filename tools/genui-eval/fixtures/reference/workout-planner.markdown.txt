### The workout

**Warm up for 3–5 minutes** first: march on the spot, swing your arms, roll your shoulders. Then do the six exercises one after the other, resting 30–60 seconds between them. That's one round.

**Cool down** with 2–3 minutes of easy walking and gentle stretches for your legs, chest and back.

### Three rules that make it stick

1. **Start easier than you think you need to.** Finishing week 1 feeling like you could do more is exactly right.
2. **Consistency beats intensity.** Three calm workouts a week do more than one heroic one followed by a week of soreness.
3. **Good pain vs bad pain.** Burning muscles are fine; sharp pain in a joint, chest pain, dizziness or unusual breathlessness mean stop. If you have a heart condition or an injury, check with a doctor first.

This covers the strength part of the [WHO](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/physical-activity) and [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/exercise/exercise-guidelines/physical-activity-guidelines-for-adults-aged-19-to-64/) guidelines (muscle-strengthening on at least 2 days a week). Add brisk walks on the other days and you're on your way to the recommended 150 minutes of moderate activity a week.
