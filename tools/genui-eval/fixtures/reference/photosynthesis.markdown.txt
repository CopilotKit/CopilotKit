Imagine if you could make your own lunch just by standing in the sun. Plants do exactly that, and the trick is called **photosynthesis**. The word means "putting together with light": *photo* = light, *synthesis* = putting together.

### 🌱 The three ingredients

A plant needs three things, and it collects each one in a different way:

1. **Sunlight ☀️** is the energy. A green substance in the leaves called **chlorophyll** catches it. Chlorophyll is also what makes leaves green.
2. **Water 💧** comes up from the soil through the roots, travels up the stem and into the leaves, a bit like drinking through a straw.
3. **Carbon dioxide 🌬️** is a gas in the air, the same one we breathe out. It gets into the leaf through tiny holes on the underside called **stomata**.

### 🏭 What happens inside the leaf

Think of every leaf as a little solar-powered kitchen. It uses the sunlight's energy to cook water and carbon dioxide into **sugar** (called glucose). The plant uses that sugar as food to grow roots, leaves, flowers and fruit. Left over is **oxygen**, which the plant lets out into the air, and that's the oxygen we breathe!

### ✍️ The recipe in one line

> carbon dioxide + water + light energy → sugar + oxygen

Scientists write it like this. You don't need to memorise it, but it's fun to see that everything is balanced: six of each go in, and six oxygen molecules come out.
