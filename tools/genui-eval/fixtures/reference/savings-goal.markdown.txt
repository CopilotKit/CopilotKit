**Interest helps a little, not a lot.** Over a few years the effect is small: at €300 a month in a savings account paying 2% a year, you'd get there in 33 months instead of 34. The habit matters far more than the rate.

**To make it stick:** set up a standing order for the day after payday, so the money moves before you can spend it, and keep it in a separate account you don't see every day.
