### What stands out

- **Rent is the big one** at 48.6%. A common rule of thumb is to keep housing near 30% of take-home pay, so rent is where the real pressure is, but it's also the hardest thing to change quickly.
- **Essentials take 1,770** (rent, food and transport), or 71.7% of the total.
- **Fun is modest** at 200, about 8% of your money. There isn't much to squeeze there.
- **Saving 500 a month is solid:** that's 6,000 a year and a 20.2% savings rate.
