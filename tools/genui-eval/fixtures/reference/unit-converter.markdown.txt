**20 °C is 68 °F**, a pleasant room temperature or a mild spring day.

### How to convert it yourself

Multiply the Celsius value by 1.8 (that's 9/5), then add 32:

**°F = °C × 1.8 + 32**

For 20 °C: 20 × 1.8 = 36, and 36 + 32 = **68 °F**.

**Quick mental version:** double the number, knock off a tenth, then add 32. For 20: double it to 40, minus 4 is 36, plus 32 is 68. If you only need a rough idea, "double it and add 30" gets you to 70 °F, close enough for deciding on a jacket.

### Handy reference points

| °C | °F | What it feels like |
|---|---|---|
| −40 | −40 | The one point where both scales agree |
| 0 | 32 | Water freezes |
| 10 | 50 | Cool autumn day |
| 20 | 68 | Room temperature |
| 25 | 77 | Warm summer day |
| 30 | 86 | Hot |
| 37 | 98.6 | Normal body temperature |
| 100 | 212 | Water boils at sea level |

**Going the other way:** subtract 32, then divide by 1.8. So 68 °F − 32 = 36, and 36 ÷ 1.8 = 20 °C.
