**What that means in practice**
- **Autumn and early winter are the wettest:** October to January each bring 57 to 67 mm over about 11 rainy days.
- **Spring is the driest stretch.** March averages under 40 mm.
- **Summer has the fewest rainy days** (about 8 a month), but the rain often comes as short, heavy showers or thunderstorms, so August is wetter than you'd expect.

London's rainy image comes from how often the sky is grey and drizzly, not from how much water falls. On average it rains properly on about one day in three, so pack an umbrella whatever the month, but you'll rarely need it all day.
